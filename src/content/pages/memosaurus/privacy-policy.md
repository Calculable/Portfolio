# Memosaurus - App: <br>Privacy Policy

This policy was last updated on March 5, 2023

If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the service.

<strong>We do not collect personal information using the app.</strong>

We do not collect, use, save or have access to any of your personal data recoded in the app “Memosaurus“ for iOS, iPad OS and macOS.

You can choose to export your topics and units to a file and share it with anyone you want.

<strong>iCloud</strong>

Your recorded topics and units are synchronized with iCloud if you have iCloud enabled on your device. iCloud is a service provided by Apple. We do not have access to your iCloud account, and your data is encrypted when it is transmitted to and from iCloud. <a href="http://support.apple.com/kb/ht4865">Learn more about privacy and security on iCloud here</a>.

<strong>Links to webpages</strong>

You can connect custom links / urls to a unit. If you click on a unit, you will be redirected to this third-party link. Note that we have no control over the content of those custom webpages and on how they collect your data. This also applies to the example units that are automatically added when you open the app for the first time. Therefore, we take no responsibility for the content or the privacy of those webpages. Please check the privacy policies of the websites you add to Memosaurus.

<strong>In-app purchases</strong>

In-app purchases are processed by the app store and not by Memosaurus itself. We have no access to your credit card information or other identifiable information. <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/">Learn more about App Store Privacy here</a>.

<strong>App Analytics</strong>

To help us understand how Memosaurus is used, we use analytics information provided by App Store Connect. This analytics information contains generic usage, transaction data and other statistics. This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you. <a href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT202100">Learn more about app analytics</a>.

<strong>Changes</strong>

This privacy policy may change in the future. We will inform you about changes by updating the “last updated“ section on this page. We encourage you to check this page regularly.

<strong>Contact</strong>

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out by <a href="/memosaurus/contact">using the contact form</a>.
