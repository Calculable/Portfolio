# Still Remember? - App: <br>Privacy Policy

<strong>We do not collect personal information using the app.</strong>

We do not collect, use, save or have access to any of your personal data recoded in the app “Still Remember?“ for iOS, iPad OS and macOS.

All your recorded memories stay locally on your device.

Please note that if you loose access to your device, there is no way to restore your recorded memories and pictures associated with your memories.

You can choose to export your memories to a file and share it with anyone you want.

If you create a new memory and want to add a photo to the memory, you will be asked to provide access to your photo library. This is only for the purpose of adding the photo to your memory. The photos will only be used locally on your device and we don’t have any access to it.

<strong>App Analytics</strong>

To help us understand how the Still Remember? - App is used, we use analytics information provided by App Store Connect. This analytics information contains generic usage, transaction data and other statistics. This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you.

<strong>Contact</strong>

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out by <a href="/remember-app/contact">using the contact form</a>.
