# Pin Puma - App: <br>Privacy Policy

This policy was last updated on September 22, 2026.

<strong>We do not collect any information using the app.</strong>

Pin Puma does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information. We do not have access to any data you record in the app.

<strong>Contact and Support</strong>

If you choose to contact us through the contact form, you provide your name, email address, and message so that we can respond to your request. The form is processed by our contact form provider, Basin. This information is provided voluntarily through the website and is separate from your use of the app.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out by <a href="/pin-puma/contact">using the contact form</a>.
